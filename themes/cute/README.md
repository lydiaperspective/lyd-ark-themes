# Lyd Ark Cute

A clean, cheerful, pastel KDE Plasma theme with pink as its primary colour and lavender as a secondary accent. The inspiration is kawaii and Sanrio-like in mood, without copying protected characters or artwork.

## Design direction

- Bubblegum and blush pink as primary colours.
- Cream-white surfaces with soft lavender accents.
- Clean, compact presentation with restrained decoration.
- Breeze remains the reference for layout, spacing, widget sizing, and control geometry.
- Keep hearts, flowers, and character-like artwork primarily in wallpapers rather than throughout the interface.
- Use coordinated lavender folder icons as a secondary visual accent.

## Status

Planned. Wicca is the first theme being implemented.
